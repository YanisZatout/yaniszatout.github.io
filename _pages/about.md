I am a Doctor of Mechanical Engineering specializing in High-Performance Computing (HPC), Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), and Scientific Machine Learning (SciML). I earned my Ph.D. from Université de Perpignan Via Domitia (PROMES & LISN). My doctoral thesis, defended on December 11, 2025, focused on the simulation and learning of turbulent highly anisothermal flows.

### Research Focus

My primary research field is the simulation and analysis of turbulent flows characterized by high temperature gradients. This work applies directly to the design and thermodynamics of next-generation solar receivers for pressurized air solar towers. My objectives are divided into three main areas:

* **Thermal-Large Eddy Simulation (T-LES):** I conduct *a posteriori* error quantification and test functional and mixed closure models to improve performance for high-temperature gradient compressible flows.
* **Flow Reconstruction:** I develop machine learning and deep learning algorithms to invert the LES filter, allowing for the accurate recovery of temperature statistics that are typically lost in filtering.
* **Flow Control:** I utilize Direct Numerical Simulation (DNS) to study spanwise wall oscillations, measuring their exact effects on boundary heat flux and skin friction under varying physical conditions.

### Technical Expertise

* **Programming & Frameworks:** C/C++, Python, Bash/Zsh, TensorFlow/Keras, PyTorch, MPI, and PETSc.
* **HPC Management:** I have supervised massively parallel computing operations, executing approximately 80 million scalar CPU hours on the European supercomputer Irene (TGCC).
* **Data Architecture:** I manage large databases (up to 120 TB) and develop custom, parallelized Python post-processing libraries designed specifically for handling large CFD datasets.

### Future Avenues

Moving forward, my goals lie in applied mathematics for turbulence research. I am focusing on developing lightweight neural network architectures for LES mixed-model constant balancing, training energy-based models for flow reconstruction, and developing reduced-order models to improve resolution time on reactive flows. 

### Teaching & Mentorship

During my doctoral research, I supervised three master's level interns, guiding them through complex thermal and chemical engineering problems. I view technical mentorship as a core component of research and intend to continue supervising students in my future roles.
